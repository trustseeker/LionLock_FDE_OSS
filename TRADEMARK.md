# LionLock name and logo policy

The LionLock **code** is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). You may use, change
and redistribute it, including commercially, under that licence.

The LionLock **name and logo** are a separate matter. Section 6 of the Apache License does not
grant permission to use them, except to describe where the software came from. This page says what
you may do with the name without asking, and what needs permission.

"LionLock" is used by the project's maintainer as an unregistered trademark (LionLock™) for this
software. Nothing on this page claims a registration.

## Why this policy exists

LionLock's value is that a result can be trusted: the same input gives the same output, and the
proof can be re-run. If modified software is distributed under the same name, users can no longer
tell whether the result they are looking at comes from the verified code. The name is how people
know which software they have.

## What you may do without asking

- **Say what you use.** State truthfully that your product, service or research uses, is built on,
  or is compatible with LionLock. For example: "Scored with LionLock", "Acme Gateway, built on
  LionLock", "a plugin for LionLock".
- **Redistribute unmodified releases** under the LionLock name, with the `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`
  files intact.
- **Write about it.** Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews, comparisons and
  academic work.
- **Name the dependency.** Refer to the `lionlock` package in code, documentation, package
  metadata and dependency lists.
- **Keep the name in a fork while you are working toward contributing back**, as long as the fork
  is not presented or distributed as the official project.

## What needs written permission

- **Distributing modified software under the LionLock name.** If you change scoring, gating,
  thresholds, weights or the audit log and distribute the result, give it a different name. You
  may say it is "based on LionLock" or "a fork of LionLock".
- **Using LionLock in the name of your own product, service, company, package or domain.** For
  example "LionLock Pro", "LionLock Cloud" or a `lionlock-*` package that is not part of this
  project. Use the form "X for LionLock" or "X, built on LionLock" instead.
- **Claiming certification or endorsement.** Do not say or imply that your product is "LionLock
  certified", "LionLock verified" or approved by the project unless you have written permission.
- **Using the logo** on your own product, packaging or marketing, other than to link to this
  project.
- **Merchandise** carrying the name or logo for sale.

## Stating results honestly

If you publish a LionLock result from a modified build, say that it is modified. A determinism
fingerprint means something only when it comes from the unmodified harness in this repository; see
[`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) and [`harness/README.md`](harness/README.md).

## How to refer to the project

- Write the name as **LionLock**, one word, with a capital L twice.
- Use it as a name, not as a verb or a generic term for trust scoring.
- On first prominent use in marketing material, write LionLock™.

## Asking for permission, or reporting misuse

Open an issue in this repository's issue tracker with "trademark" in the title, describing the use
you have in mind. Permission is given in writing by the maintainer, in that issue.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The version that applies is the one on the `main` branch of this
repository. It does not change or limit any right you have under the Apache License 2.0 to the
code itself.
